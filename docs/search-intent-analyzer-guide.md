# How to Use the Search Intent Analyzer

The Search Intent Analyzer helps you determine whether your page matches what people are actually searching for. Google ranks pages that align with search intent, not just pages with keywords. This tool shows you how well your page matches your target keyword's intent.

## What is Search Intent?

Search intent is the goal behind a search query. Google categorizes intent into types:

- **Informational** - Users want to learn something (e.g., "how to improve seo")
- **Transactional** - Users want to buy something (e.g., "best seo tools")
- **Commercial** - Users are researching before buying (e.g., "seo tool reviews")
- **Navigational** - Users want to find a specific site (e.g., "seoaxe")
- **Tool-Seeking** - Users want a tool to solve a problem (e.g., "free seo checker")

If your page doesn't match the intent, you won't rank regardless of keyword density.

## How to Use the Search Intent Analyzer

1. Go to [seoaxe.site](https://www.seoaxe.site)
2. Click "Login" and sign in to your account
3. Navigate to the **Intent** page or go to `/app` and select the **Intent** tool

3. Click **"Check Intent Match"**

### Step 3: Review Your Results

The analyzer displays:

- **Overall Score** (0-100) - How well your page matches the intent
- **Intent Type** - The detected intent category
- **Category Scores** - Breakdown by element:
  - Title Tag Match
  - H1 Heading Match
  - First 100 Words Match
  - Body Content Match
  - Meta Description Match
  - Subheadings Match

### Step 4: Understand the Checks

The tool checks for keyword presence in key locations:

- **Title Tag** - Is the keyword in your page title?
- **H1 Heading** - Is the keyword in your main heading?
- **First 100 Words** - Does the keyword appear early in the content?
- **Body Content** - Overall keyword usage throughout
- **Meta Description** - Is the keyword in the meta description?
- **Subheadings** - Are related terms in H2/H3 headings?

Green checkmarks indicate the element is optimized; red X marks mean it needs work.

### Step 5: Read the Recommendations

The tool provides specific recommendations based on your score:

- "Add the exact keyword to your title tag"
- "Include the keyword in your H1 heading"
- "Use the keyword in the first 100 words"
- "Add H2 subheadings with the keyword"
- "Include transactional cues" (for buy keywords)
- "Structure as a guide/tutorial" (for how-to keywords)

### Step 6: Fix the Issues

Based on the recommendations:

1. **Update your title tag** to include the exact keyword
2. **Rewrite your H1** to match the search query
3. **Move keyword earlier** in your introduction
4. **Add subheadings** that include the keyword or related terms
5. **Adjust content structure** based on intent type

### Step 7: Re-Check

After making changes, run the analyzer again to verify improvement.

## Intent-Specific Tips

### Informational Intent (How-to guides)
- Start with "How to" or "What is"
- Use step-by-step structure
- Include examples and screenshots
- Add FAQ section
- Target "guide" or "tutorial" keywords

### Transactional Intent (Buying)
- Include pricing information
- Add "buy" or "get" CTAs
- Show product features
- Include testimonials
- Target "best" or "cheap" keywords

### Commercial Intent (Research)
- Compare options
- Include pros/cons tables
- Add review sections
- Target "review" or "vs" keywords

### Tool-Seeking Intent (Free tools)
- Emphasize "free" in title and content
- Add immediate access CTA
- Highlight key features
- Target "free" or "checker" keywords

## What the Score Means

- **80-100**: Strong intent match - Your page signals clearly match the search query
- **50-79**: Moderate match - A few adjustments could significantly improve rankings
- **0-49**: Weak match - The page may not rank well without major changes

## Common Mistakes

1. **Targeting wrong intent** - Writing a sales page for an informational query
2. **Missing keyword in title/H1** - Most important signals
3. **Keyword appears too late** - First 100 words are crucial
4. **Wrong content structure** - Tutorial format for commercial queries
5. **No transactional signals** - Missing CTAs for buy keywords

## Tips for Better Results

- **Check competitors** - Analyze top-ranking pages to see their intent signals
- **Match the SERP** - Look at what Google shows for your keyword
- **Use exact keyword** - Don't vary the keyword in title/H1
- **Think like a searcher** - What would satisfy someone searching this?
- **Test different intents** - If one keyword doesn't work, try another

## Common Questions

**Q: Why is my score low even with the keyword?**
A: Intent is about more than keywords. Check if your content structure matches what searchers want.

**Q: Can I change intent type?**
A: Yes, rewrite your content to match a different intent, but it's often better to target a different keyword.

**Q: How important is first 100 words?**
A: Very important. Google checks early content to determine relevance.

**Q: Should I stuff keywords?**
A: No. Use the keyword naturally in key locations, but focus on matching intent, not density.
