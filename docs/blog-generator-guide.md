# How to Use the Blog Generator

The Blog Generator creates SEO-optimized blog posts at scale from just a list of keywords. It's designed for content velocity - generating 20-50 high-quality blog posts quickly to build topical authority and capture organic traffic.

## What the Blog Generator Does

The generator creates:
- Full blog posts (~1500 words each)
- SEO-optimized titles with keywords
- Compelling meta descriptions (under 160 characters)
- Proper H2/H3 heading structure
- FAQ sections with schema markup
- HTML-ready content
- URL slugs for publishing

## Keyword Strategy

### Choosing Keywords

**Long-Tail Keywords**
- 4+ words
- Lower competition
- Higher conversion intent
- Examples: "how to improve domain authority in 2026", "best seo tools for small business south africa"

**Question-Based Keywords**
- Start with "how to", "what is", "why"
- Match search intent
- Great for informational content
- Examples: "how to get backlinks fast", "what is e-e-a-t"

**Problem-Solution Keywords**
- Address specific pain points
- High intent
- Good for commercial content
- Examples: "why my website dropped in rankings", "fix 404 errors"

**Current Year Keywords**
- Include current year (2026)
- Shows content is fresh
- Better for time-sensitive topics
- Examples: "seo trends 2026", "best wordpress plugins 2026"

### Content Clusters

Generate related posts to build topical authority:
- **Pillar content** - Comprehensive guide on a topic
- **Cluster content** - Supporting articles on subtopics
- **Internal linking** - Link cluster content to pillar

Example cluster for "SEO":
- Pillar: "Complete SEO Guide for 2026"
- Cluster: "How to improve domain authority", "Best SEO tools", "SEO for beginners"
