# How to Use the Content Gap Detector

The Content Gap Analyzer identifies topics and keywords your competitors are ranking for that you're not. By finding these content gaps, you can create new content to capture additional organic traffic and outrank your competition.

## What is a Content Gap?

A content gap is a topic or keyword that:
- Your competitors rank for
- You don't rank for
- Represents an opportunity to create new content
- Could bring you additional traffic

Content gaps occur when competitors have covered topics you haven't addressed yet.

## How to Use the Content Gap Detector

### Step 1: Access the Tool

1. Go to [seoaxe.site](https://www.seoaxe.site)
2. Click "Login" and sign in to your account
3. Navigate to `/app` and select the **"Missing Content"** tab

### Step 2: Enter Your Domain

1. Enter your website domain
   - Example: `https://www.yoursite.com`
2. Enter competitor domains (up to 5)
   - Example: `https://www.competitor1.com`, `https://www.competitor2.com`
3. Click **"Analyze Content Gaps"**

### Step 3: Review the Analysis

The tool displays:

**Gap Summary**
- Total keywords competitors rank for
- Keywords you're missing
- Estimated traffic opportunity
- Priority gaps to target

**Gap Categories**
- **High Volume** - Keywords with significant search volume
- **Low Competition** - Easier to rank for
- **High Intent** - Transactional or commercial intent
- **Quick Wins** - Low-hanging fruit opportunities

**Detailed Gap List**
Each gap shows:
- **Keyword** - The search term
- **Search Volume** - Monthly search volume
- **Competition** - Difficulty level (Low, Medium, High)
- **Competitors** - Which competitors rank for it
- **Intent Type** - Informational, Transactional, Commercial
- **Suggested Content** - Type of content to create

### Step 4: Prioritize Gaps

Focus on gaps with the best opportunity:

**Quick Wins (Target First)**
- Low competition

- Match your business offering

**High Impact**
- High search volume
- Relevant to your audience
- Within your content capabilities

**Strategic Gaps**
- Low competition but growing volume

- Long-term brand building

## Understanding Gap Categories

### High Volume Gaps
Keywords with 1,000+ monthly searches. These can bring significant traffic but are usually competitive. Best for:
- Established sites with authority
- Comprehensive, long-form content
- Multiple content pieces targeting the topic

### Low Competition Gaps
Keywords few competitors are targeting. These are easier to rank for. Best for:
- Newer sites building authority
- Quick wins to build momentum
- Niche, specific topics

### High Intent Gaps
Transactional or commercial keywords. These convert well. Best for:
- Product pages
- Landing pages
- Bottom-of-funnel content

### Quick Wins
Gaps that are easy to target and can show results quickly. Best for:
- Building initial momentum
- Demonstrating SEO value
- Low-effort, high-impact content

## Best Practices

### Choosing Competitors
- **Direct competitors** - Companies in your niche
- **Top rankers** - Sites ranking for your target keywords
- **Content leaders** - Sites known for great content
- **Mix of sizes** - Both large and small competitors

### Analyzing Gaps
- **Relevance first** - Only target gaps relevant to your business
- **Consider resources** - Can you create quality content for this topic?
- **Check intent** - Does the intent match your offering?
- **Evaluate competition** - Can you realistically outrank them?

### Creating Content
- **Go deep** - Create more comprehensive content than competitors
- **Be unique** - Add your unique perspective or data
- **Optimize properly** - Use SEO tools to optimize
- **Promote** - Don't just publish - promote your content

## Common Mistakes

**Targeting Irrelevant Gaps**
- Problem: Creating content for topics outside your expertise
- Solution: Only target gaps relevant to your business and audience

**Creating Thin Content**
- Problem: Publishing low-quality content just to fill gaps
- Solution: Create comprehensive, valuable content

**Ignoring Competition**
- Problem: Not checking if you can realistically rank
- Solution: Evaluate competitor strength before creating content

**Not Tracking Results**
- Problem: Creating content without measuring impact
- Solution: Track rankings, traffic, and conversions

**One-and-Done**
- Problem: Publishing content and never updating
- Solution: Update and improve content over time

## Common Questions

**Q: How many gaps should I target?**
A: Start with 5-10 quick wins, then expand to high-impact gaps.

**Q: What if I can't create content for a gap?**
A: Skip it or hire a writer. Don't create low-quality content just to fill a gap.

**Q: How long until I see results?**
A: Typically 3-6 months for ranking improvements, depending on competition and content quality.

**Q: Should I target high-competition gaps?**
A: Only if you have the authority and resources to create exceptional content that can compete.
