# How to Use the SEO Audit Tool

The SEO Audit Tool analyzes your website's SEO health and provides actionable recommendations to improve your search rankings. This guide explains how to use it effectively.

## What the SEO Audit Tool Checks

The audit evaluates five key areas:

1. **Meta Tags & Title** - Title length, meta description, heading structure
2. **Content Quality** - Word count, keyword usage, readability
3. **Technical SEO** - Page speed, mobile-friendliness, HTTPS
4. **Social Media** - Open Graph and Twitter Card tags
5. **Links** - Internal and external link structure

Each area receives a score from 0-100, and you get an overall SEO health score.

## How to Use the SEO Audit Tool

### Step 1: Access the Tool

1. Go to [seoaxe.site](https://www.seoaxe.site)
2. Click "Login" and sign in to your account
3. Navigate to the **Audit** page or go to `/app` and select the **Audit** tool

### Step 2: Enter Your URL

1. In the "Your page URL" field, enter the full URL of the page you want to audit
2. Example: `https://www.yoursite.com/your-page`
3. Click the **"Check SEO Score"** button

### Step 3: Review Your Results

The audit will analyze your page and display:

- **Overall Score** - A gauge showing your total SEO health (0-100)
- **Category Scores** - Individual scores for Meta, Content, Technical, Social, and Links
- **Detailed Findings** - Specific issues with severity indicators (Critical, Warning, Info)

### Step 4: Understand the Findings

Each finding includes:
- **Severity** - Red (Critical), Yellow (Warning), or Blue (Info)
- **Description** - What the issue is
- **Recommendation** - How to fix it

Common issues you might see:
- "Title tag is missing or too short"
- "Meta description exceeds 160 characters"
- "H1 heading not found"
- "No Open Graph tags detected"
- "Page has no internal links"

### Step 5: Fix the Issues

Start with critical issues first:
1. Add or optimize your title tag (50-60 characters)
2. Write a compelling meta description (150-160 characters)
3. Ensure you have an H1 heading with your main keyword
4. Add Open Graph and Twitter Card tags for social sharing
5. Build internal links between your pages

### Step 6: Re-Audit

After making changes, run the audit again to verify improvements and track your progress over time.

## Tips for Better Results

- **Audit key pages first** - Homepage, product pages, and top blog posts
- **Compare with competitors** - Audit their pages to see what they're doing differently
- **Track progress** - Save your audit scores to measure improvement
- **Focus on high-impact fixes** - Title, meta description, and H1 have the biggest impact

## What the Score Means

- **80-100**: Excellent SEO - Your page is well-optimized
- **50-79**: Good SEO - Room for improvement
- **0-49**: Needs work - Focus on critical issues first
